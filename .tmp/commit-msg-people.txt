Anti-pattern: `<a href="javascript:history.back()">` is hostile to
accessibility, server-side rendering, and any CSP that doesn't allow
javascript: URIs. Replaced with a real <button> that calls
history.back() in an onclick handler — semantically correct, works
under any CSP, and screen readers announce it correctly as a button.

Same pattern already used elsewhere in the codebase for non-link
back-actions.
