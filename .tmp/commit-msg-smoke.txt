ci(testing): add built-bundle CSP smoke check (closes #67)

Adds tools/built-bundle-smoke.cjs, a Node script that builds the Svelte
SPA into server/internal/webapp/static-next, compiles xuva, boots it
against a temp data dir on a non-default port, polls /api/health, and
asserts Content-Security-Policy img-src permits https: (or the known
artwork hosts). Wires it into .github/workflows/security.yml as a
parallel job that runs on PR + push to main. Verified locally: success
path exits 0; deliberate removal of `https:` from CSP exits 1 with a
clear error naming the missing artwork hosts.
