feat(search): broaden library search to people and collections

Adds GET /api/client/search?q=&limit= returning movies, series, people
(from metadata cast/crew), and collections (from movie metadata
$.collection) in one shot. Results are scored exact > prefix >
word-prefix > contains and capped at limit per type (default 8, max 40).

The search store now calls the backend with a 150ms debounce instead of
loading the full catalogue on prime. Header dropdown and the /search
page render person avatars and collection posters alongside movies and
series, linking to /people/{name} and /collections/{id}.
